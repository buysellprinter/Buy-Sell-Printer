# BuySellPrinter (BSP)

**Buy with confidence. Sell with ease.**

BuySellPrinter (BSP) is an upcoming digital platform focused on the production printing industry.

We are building a dedicated online destination to make it easier for businesses, print shops, dealers and industry professionals to discover production printers, explore machine-related services and connect with relevant opportunities.

## What is BuySellPrinter?

The used production printing market often relies on personal networks, phone calls and scattered online listings. Finding the right machine or reaching the right buyer can take time, and important machine information may be difficult to compare.

BSP aims to bring more structure, transparency and convenience to this process.

## What BSP is being built to support

- **Production printers:** Discover used and refurbished production printing equipment.
- **Machine listings:** Help buyers and sellers connect around available machines and requirements.
- **Parts and components:** Make it easier to find relevant production printer parts.
- **Engineers and services:** Connect businesses with technical professionals and service options.
- **Industry knowledge:** Share useful information about machine selection, maintenance, troubleshooting and production printing.

## Our approach

We believe that buying or selling a production printer should involve clear information and informed decisions.

Our long-term vision is to make machine transactions more transparent through better documentation, relevant machine details, inspection information where available, and a clearer transaction process.

Verification and inspection features will depend on the services implemented and available for each transaction.

## Who BSP is for

- Commercial print shops
- Digital printing businesses
- Production printer dealers
- Used and refurbished machine sellers
- Printing equipment engineers
- Spare parts suppliers
- Businesses looking to buy or sell production printing equipment
