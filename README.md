# BuySellPrinter (BSP)

**Buy with confidence. Sell with ease.**

BuySellPrinter (BSP) is an upcoming digital platform being built for the production printing industry. Its long-term vision is to help print businesses, machine buyers, sellers, dealers, engineers and parts suppliers connect through a more organized online experience.

**Website:** [Visit BuySellPrinter](http://www.buysellprinter.com)

> **Project status:** Under development. Features described in this repository are planned or part of the project vision unless explicitly stated otherwise on the live website.

## Why BSP?

Buying or selling a used production printer is different from buying an ordinary consumer product. Machines can be expensive, technical, difficult to compare remotely, and located far from the buyer. Information may be spread across phone calls, messages, dealer networks and separate listings.

BSP aims to make the process easier to navigate by bringing relevant machine information, industry services and knowledge into one dedicated place.

## What BSP is being built to support

- **Production printer discovery:** Explore used and refurbished production printing equipment.
- **Machine listings and requirements:** Help buyers and sellers find relevant opportunities.
- **Parts and components:** Make it easier to search for production printer parts.
- **Engineers and services:** Help businesses discover technical and service support.
- **Industry knowledge:** Provide useful information about machine selection, maintenance and troubleshooting.
- **More transparent transactions:** Work toward clearer machine details, documented observations and a better-defined transaction process where services are available.

## Our principles

1. **Clear information:** Make relevant details easier to understand.
2. **Evidence over assumptions:** Distinguish verified information from seller-provided claims and information that is not yet checked.
3. **Realistic expectations:** An inspection can describe a machine's observed condition at a point in time; it cannot guarantee that a used machine will never need repairs.
4. **Useful industry focus:** Build for the needs of the production printing community rather than for generic classifieds alone.

## Who BSP is for

- Commercial and digital print shops
- Production printer buyers and sellers
- Used and refurbished machine dealers
- Printing equipment engineers and service professionals
- Spare-parts suppliers
- Businesses researching production printing equipment
