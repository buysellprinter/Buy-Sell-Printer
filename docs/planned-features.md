# BuySellPrinter: Planned Platform Areas

This document describes areas BSP is being built to support. It does not mean every feature is live today. Availability will be communicated on the website as development progresses.

## 1. Production printer discovery

A dedicated place to discover used and refurbished production printing equipment, with relevant machine details to help users assess suitability.

Potential listing information may include model, location, configuration, counter evidence, photographs, accessories, condition notes and known issues where supplied or checked.

## 2. Buyer requirements and seller listings

The goal is to help buyers communicate what they need and help sellers present available machines to relevant prospects.

## 3. Parts and components

A planned area to help users identify parts by machine model, part number or description and find relevant suppliers.

## 4. Engineers and service professionals

A planned discovery area for technical professionals and service options, such as troubleshooting, maintenance, inspection, installation and machine relocation, subject to actual provider coverage.

## 5. Industry knowledge and troubleshooting

Educational material about production printer selection, maintenance, common issues and technical terminology. Troubleshooting information should be treated as general guidance, not a substitute for qualified service where needed.

## 6. More organized transaction support

The long-term vision includes a clearer process for communicating machine details, inspection observations, documents, logistics and transaction stages where BSP offers those services.

Any verification label should explain what was checked, by whom and when. Inspection is a point-in-time assessment, not a promise of future fault-free operation.

## Feature status

BSP is under development. Planned capabilities may change as the platform evolves. Please refer to the live website for current availability.
