# The BuySellPrinter (BSP) Mission

**Buy with confidence. Sell with ease.**

## Our purpose

BuySellPrinter (BSP) is being built around a simple idea: the production printing industry deserves a dedicated online platform that makes it easier to discover equipment, find relevant services and navigate used-machine opportunities.

Production printers are specialized business assets. Buyers need to understand specifications, configuration, condition and support requirements. Sellers need to reach relevant buyers without relying exclusively on scattered conversations and personal networks.

## The problem we want to address

Information about used production printers can be fragmented across listings, dealers, messages and industry contacts. This can make it harder to compare machines, understand what information has been checked and coordinate the steps involved in a transaction.

The exact experience varies by seller, machine and transaction. BSP's aim is to make the process more organized and information-led.

## Our approach

We aim to bring together:

- Production printer listings and buyer requirements
- Information that helps users compare machines
- Access to parts, engineers and service providers
- Educational content about equipment and the printing industry
- A clearer process for machine enquiries and transactions, as capabilities become available

## Our principles

### Be clear about what is known
Information supplied by a seller should not be confused with information independently verified by BSP.

### Explain the limits of inspection
A machine inspection records observations at a particular time. It does not eliminate the risk of future faults or repairs.

### Design for both sides
Buyers need useful information; sellers need a practical way to present their equipment and connect with relevant prospects.

### Build for the industry
BSP focuses on production printing equipment and its surrounding ecosystem, including parts, engineers and technical knowledge.

## Our long-term vision

We want BuySellPrinter to become a useful digital destination for the production printing community—not simply a place to post a listing, but a platform that helps people find information, discover relevant opportunities and make more informed decisions.
