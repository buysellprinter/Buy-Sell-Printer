# BuySellPrinter FAQ

## What is BuySellPrinter (BSP)?

BuySellPrinter is an upcoming digital platform focused on the production printing industry, including used and refurbished production printers and related industry services.

## Is BSP launched?

BSP is under development. Check the official website for the latest status and currently available features.

## What kinds of equipment is BSP focused on?

The project's niche is production printing equipment used by commercial and digital print businesses. Exact listing coverage will depend on the equipment and services available on the platform.

## Will BSP include parts and engineers?

Parts discovery and engineer/service discovery are part of the platform vision. Availability will depend on the features and provider coverage launched.

## Does “refurbished” mean the machine is like new?

No. The term can mean different things depending on the seller and the work performed. Buyers should ask for details of refurbishment, testing, replaced components and any written terms.

## Does inspection guarantee that a machine will never fail?

No. An inspection records observations at a point in time and cannot guarantee future performance or eliminate the possibility of repairs.

## Can a seller list a machine on BSP today?

Please check the official website for the current launch status and any available registration or enquiry process.
